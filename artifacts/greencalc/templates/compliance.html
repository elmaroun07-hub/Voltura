Transparency documentation for the GreenCalc AI system in accordance with the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689).

📋 Model Card

Model Type

RandomForestRegressor (scikit-learn) — a supervised ensemble learning method using 50 decision trees with max depth 10.

Purpose

Predicts hourly household energy consumption (kWh) based on temporal and environmental features. Used to contextualize energy saving estimates within the GreenCalc solar calculator.

Training Data

Synthetic dataset of 8,760 hourly records generated using physical energy consumption models. Features are based on established energy usage patterns from residential buildings in temperate climates. No personally identifiable information (PII) is used.

Input Features

  • month (1–12): Month of the year
  • hour (0–23): Hour of the day
  • irradiance (kWh/m²/day): Solar irradiance from NASA POWER
  • temperature (°C): Ambient temperature from NASA POWER

Output

Predicted energy consumption in kWh for the given hour.

Limitations & Risks

  • Trained on synthetic data — predictions may differ from real-world consumption
  • Does not account for household size, appliance mix, or occupant behavior
  • Performance may degrade for locations with very different climate profiles
  • Not intended as the primary basis for financial decisions

🔍 Transparency Statement

Classification

GreenCalc is classified as a limited-risk AI system under the EU AI Act. It provides energy calculations and predictions to assist users in making informed decisions about solar energy investments.

Human Oversight

All predictions are advisory. The system does not make autonomous decisions. Users retain full control over any financial or installation decisions.

Data Processing

User inputs (location, energy usage, system specifications) are processed locally and stored in a local SQLite database. No personal data is shared with third parties. Location data is geocoded via Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) and used only to query the NASA POWER API.

Accuracy & Reliability

The 8760-hour simulation uses NumPy-vectorized physically grounded equations. The ML consumption model is supplementary and explicitly labeled as a prediction. Results should be validated against actual system specifications and local installer quotes.

⚖️ Right to Explanation

In accordance with Article 13 of the EU AI Act, users have the right to understand how the AI system arrived at its outputs. GreenCalc provides:

  • SHAP values for every ML prediction (see section above and the SHAP API endpoint)
  • Full monthly energy simulation breakdown on every results page
  • Transparent formula: ROI uses 1.5% annual inflation over 25 years
  • Carbon intensity data source: country-specific grid mix (JSON, openly auditable)
  • NumPy-vectorized simulation code is deterministic for identical inputs

🍪 Privacy Notice

GreenCalc stores your calculation results (energy savings, CO₂, costs) to show history and improve the service. This is necessary for the app to function.

Your location coordinates (lat/lon) are used only for the current session to fetch NASA solar data. They are not stored in our database unless you opt in on the calculator form.

You can delete all your data at any time via Delete my data.

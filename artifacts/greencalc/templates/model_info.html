🎯 System Purpose & Intended Use

GreenCalc estimates the optimal residential PV wattage for single-family homes in the EU and Southern Mediterranean region, using a gradient boosting regression model trained on synthetic solar data.

✅ Residential PV sizing (100W–20,000W) ✅ Single-family homes ✅ EU / Southern Mediterranean (lat 28°–60°) ❌ Commercial/industrial ❌ Off-grid systems ❌ lat < 28° or > 60°

⚠️ Known Limitations
  • Calibrated for residential PV only (100W–20,000W). Do not use for commercial or industrial systems.
  • Accuracy degrades outside lat 28°–60°. Nordic countries and North Africa are underrepresented in training data.
  • Wind generation uses a simplified capacity-factor model, not a full meteorological simulation.
  • Does not account for local shading, roof orientation variation, or grid connection constraints.
  • Synthetic training data may not capture all real-world variability.

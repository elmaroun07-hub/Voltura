- **3.7 — the index.** CLOSED. `lib/screening/src/heat-index.ts`, method `h3-index-v1`.
  Three judgments went to the operator (14 Oct 2026, ~01:48) with measured consequences; he
  answered "choose the best option" for each. Chosen under that delegation:
  - **Weights — risk thirds**, after IPCC AR5 WGII (risk from hazard, exposure and
    vulnerability): hazard ⅙ (July horizontal Hsol;jul of the zone), exposure ⅙, building
    sensitivity ⅓ (cooling demand), people ⅓ (over-64 share, one-person households,
    unemployment). Weighted geometric mean of factors relative to the municipality's
    residential median, so 1.0 = typical and the contract's "×" holds. Equal weights gave
    a climate term that barely varies inside one municipality the same say as people;
    building-only left the "poverty" out of summer energy poverty.
  - **One score describes the building's top-floor dwelling.** Building average let
    detached houses take 96–98 % of the top 1,000: a block's roof heat is spread over its
    floors, and the dwellings that overheat in a block are the top ones. Exposure = half
    roof (constant: every top floor has one; its sun is the hazard term) + half street
    façades per metre of perimeter. The 50/50 split is a stated assumption — storey height
    and floor plan are in no public register.
  - **Ranking on the high end of each band** (precautionary: look first where the risk could
    be highest; a survey collapses the band).
  - Found on the first full run and fixed before closing: ranking on the high end let one
    certificate reading 1,592 kWh/m²·year (municipal median 22.8) put a 16-dwelling block at
    rank 1. Certificate values are now winsorised at the municipality's p99 of all
    certificates (Córdoba 60.22); 215 buildings carry a capped certificate and are flagged.
- **3.8 — completeness and bands.** CLOSED. Hazard and exposure are points; cooling demand
  blends the building's certificates (weight = certified entries / dwellings, capped at 1)
  with its age band's p10/p50/p90 (NBE-CT-79 → 1980; CTE DB-HE → 2007); people takes the
  municipal p10–p90 when the section is missing or withheld. Every row stores its factors
  (low/mid/high), the run stores method and references, and the integration test recomputes
  every score from what is stored. `CHECK (low ≤ mid ≤ high)` on the table.
  Córdoba: 32,431 residential buildings scored (5,075 not residential, 344 with no
  dwellings); 11,695 with a certificate; 5 without census input. Top 1,000 by the high end:
  67 % blocks of 4+ floors, 32 % single dwellings, 80 % built before 1980, 81 % certified,
  13,972 dwellings. Scoring runs in 2.2 s.

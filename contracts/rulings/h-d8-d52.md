## Design decisions the block left open

1. **Field names.** `pvDirectKwh`, `batteryChargeKwh` (PV into the battery, before the charge
   loss), `batteryDischargeKwh` (delivered, after the discharge loss), `surplusKwh`; the
   engine's hourly and summed records also carry `storedKwh` and `drawnKwh`, the amounts
   either side of the two losses, which is what lets T3 check where the losses sit.
2. **The existing monthly fields are untouched**, rounding included — `savingsKwh` drives the
   money, so a change there would have moved it. The new monthly fields use the same 0.1 kWh
   rounding. A displayed row therefore balances within 4 × 0.05 kWh; T4 asserts the
   identities on the exact sums and states the display bound separately.
3. **Percentages are rounded independently**, so the three slices can sum to 99–101 (49 + 20 +
   30 = 99 at the Optimizer's sizing). Forcing 100 would make one slice not its recorded sum
   (F-e).
4. **The engine takes an optional `onHour` callback** for T1–T3; the route does not pass it.
   `simulate8760` and its types are now exported, for the tests.
5. **The year is the sum of the months**, computed after the loop, so T4's year = Σ months
   holds exactly.
6. **The recorded check reuses the response schema**: `monthlyEntrySchema` is exported from
   `shared-routes.ts` and parsed in the PDF route. One definition of "recorded".
7. **PDF: six equal columns.** "Surplus not used" measures 73.5 pt at 9 pt bold against 81.3 pt
   of room, so it does not wrap; row heights are unchanged, and a 12-month report is still 2
   pages (M1 of H-PDF passes).
8. **Web schema strict.** The four fields are required on the web, like the server. See the
   deploy-skew note in RESIDUE.
9. **Removed with the old formula:** `totalGenKwh` (read only by it) and `solarDirect`, the
   dead variable the report numbered D9. `totalGridKwh` (`routes.ts:1080`) was already unread
   before this block and is left.

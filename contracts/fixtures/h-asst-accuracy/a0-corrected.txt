Voltura is an online tool that lets homeowners model a residential solar + battery system for their specific site. It: * Finds the location you enter (via OpenStreetMap) and pulls NASA POWER's monthly solar irradiance and temperature for that point, averaged over 2019-2023; if NASA gives no usable answer, it estimates them from the latitude and labels the result as an estimate. * Simulates a full year hour-by-hour, combining the solar generation with a typical household consumption profile built from the annual kWh you provide. * Adjusts the generation for panel orientation (south, east, west, north) and tilt angle, and runs the battery charge-discharge cycle at the round-trip efficiency you set (90 % by default). * Shows you the annual energy saved (kWh), a monthly chart of generation, savings, grid import and any surplus that can't be used, plus the overall mix of Direct Solar, From Battery and Grid Import. * Estimates CO2 avoided using location-based grid factors: official data for mainland Spain and the UK, an older factor with no cited source for 27 other countries, and for anywhere else it refuses the calculation. * Values the savings for Spain and the UK only, with your own electricity tariff, or Eurostat's household average for Spain; otherwise it shows no monetary value. * Calculates a pay-back period and a 25-year ROI curve based on the installation cost you supply (or, in the UK only, a sourced PV-only cost). * Generates a downloadable PDF report with the inputs, the results and the monthly table, and keeps a history of your past runs tied to this browser. * Includes an AI Optimizer that recommends a PV size and battery capacity from your annual usage and local irradiance, using a fixed standard daily usage profile, and explains the numbers. In short, Voltura helps you predict how much solar and battery power you'll actually use, how much you'll save in Spain and the UK, and what the environmental impact will be - all for your own address and annual usage.
